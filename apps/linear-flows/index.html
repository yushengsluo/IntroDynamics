Explore your system

Every arrow shows the instantaneous direction of motion for x′ = Ax. Colored paths follow solutions from their initial conditions.

  • Edit any matrix entry or choose a preset. Representative points are generated for equilibria, invariant directions, and mixed behaviors; added points remain when you edit the matrix. Use Reset representative points to restore the examples. A finite set illustrates the different types of motion, rather than every possible orbit.
  • In 2D, click or drag on the trace–determinant plane to change the matrix and phase portrait together. Turn on Follow pointer for hover updates. Fixed sample points and all colors stay unchanged as you cross regions. Two tracked points move continuously onto the stable direction of a saddle, showing solutions that converge to the origin. Editing a tracked point makes it a fixed custom point. Reset representative points to sample invariant directions for the current matrix. The marker also follows matrix edits and presets. Curves separate nodes, spirals, saddles, and degenerate cases.
  • Click the portrait to add x(0). In 3D, choose the coordinate plane and its fixed coordinate first; the translucent plane shows where points will be placed. Drag to rotate without adding a point.
  • Switch to 3D and drag the portrait to orbit. Scroll or use + / − to zoom.
  • Set Start t and End t to explore any interval, including negative times. Initial conditions specify x(0); negative time follows the same solution backward. Play or scrub time to follow the moving points. Turn off full paths to reveal the paths as they evolve.

Eigenvalues describe the behavior near the origin. Negative real parts pull solutions inward; positive real parts cause growth; imaginary parts produce rotation.

This lab models homogeneous systems with constant real coefficients. Time is in arbitrary units. Solutions are computed with a matrix exponential; paths stop beyond the plotting limit.
